## Notes

- **Richest taxonomy** of any marketplace surveyed — 679 categories across 14 top-level groups
- Taxonomy is **two-level hierarchical**: 14 main groups with 5-13 subcategories each
- As an aggregator/directory, Datarade needs the broadest possible category coverage
- Categories are **domain-oriented** (what the data is about), not delivery-format-oriented
- Notable top-level separation: **Commerce** vs **Transaction** vs **Consumer** — these overlap conceptually but Datarade treats them as distinct
- **Trade Data** as its own top-level category (import/export, customs, logistics) is unique vs other marketplaces
- **Legal Data** as a top-level category is also unique
- Each subcategory likely has even deeper tags (679 total suggests ~3-4 levels), but the deep taxonomy was not fully extractable
- Source: https://datarade.ai/data-categories
